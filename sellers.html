For Aircraft Sellers

An independent and comprehensive aircraft condition report strengthens your sale position!

We are excited to announce a new service which is available to approved aircraft sellers!

We are offering Pre-Purchase Aircraft Inspection service to aircraft sellers on “Pay 50% Now and 50% On Sale” terms.

This would allow you to advertise your aircraft with an Independent Pre-Purchase Inspection Report and only pay 50% of our fee on completion of our service, with the balance due and payable upon sale of your aircraft.

An independent aircraft condition report strengthens your sale position and attracts uneasy buyers.

We are Melbourne based and have been conducting operations throughout Australia for two years and recently expanded our business to also offer this service in New Zealand.

If we can assist, please feel free to reach out and discuss.
