About Us

Experience and Approvals

The proprietor of Elite Airways, Roman Badov has been issued with Head of Aircraft Airworthiness and Maintenance Control (HAAMC) certification by the Civil Aviation Safety Authority (CASA).

Technicians conducting Aircraft Pre-Purchase Inspections have completed required training in accordance with Schedule 8 and Schedule 5 of the Civil Aviation Regulations, under Regulation 42ZC.

Our History

Elite Airways has operated as an aviation enterprise since 1995.
The organisation conducted aircraft charter and a variety of aerial work services under operational approvals, as issued by the Civil Aviation Safety Authority (CASA).

The proprietor's new vision is to support the Australian and New Zealand aviation industry by offering Aircraft Pre-Purchase Inspection Service.

This new business dimension evolved as a direct observation of such service to be lacking and needed.

The proprietor has the required regulatory knowledge and certification, industry experience and skill level to provide the service as described here.
