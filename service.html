Our Service

Aircraft Pre-Purchase Inspections

Elite Airways is a leader within the General Aviation industry in the conduct of Aircraft Pre-Purchase Inspections.

Elite Airways Aircraft Pre-Purchase Inspection Service is offered to prospective buyers and sellers of aircraft. This services includes a detailed review of aircraft logbooks and any accident history. Inspection of the aircraft is carried out by a duly certified technician and any defects reported. System of Maintenance for the aircraft is reviewed and based on component times remaining in service, any airworthiness directives and upcoming scheduled maintenance, an estimate will be prepared for the client regarding operating costs for the next 12 months / 500 flight hours. A pre-purchase test flight is conducted.

The client is issued with a written report regarding the aircraft's condition and is invited to have a confidential discussion with the technician who performed the inspection.

We service Australia and New Zealand
We specialise in conducting inspections of piston powered: Passenger and/or freight, fixed wing aircraft.
